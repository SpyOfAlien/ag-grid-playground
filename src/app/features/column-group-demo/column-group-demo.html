Drag headers to move columns or groups. Hold Shift at any point during a drag to highlight the column or group under the cursor, then release the mouse to group. Release Shift to resume normal movement. Drop on a group header to join it, or on a child column to create a subgroup. Hold Shift and drop a child below the headers to ungroup it. Normal left-drag keeps children together. Use Ungroup in a group header to remove that level. Enable Middle mouse grouping to use middle-drag for the same grouping and ungrouping actions without holding Shift. Drag one group header onto another while grouping to create a parent containing both groups.
